Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your online tutoring journey with Leanovia Virtual Institute.

How are lessons conducted?

Lessons are conducted live online, 1-on-1 via video conferencing tools like Zoom or Google Meet. We use interactive collaborative whiteboards where both the tutor and student can write, draw, and upload worksheets in real-time.

Who chooses the tutor for my child?

Our academic matchmaker team selects the best tutor based on the student's requirements (curriculum, subject, learning pace, and timezone). You can also request a specific gender preference (male/female tutor).

Is the demo class completely free?

Yes, the first 30-minute diagnostic/demo class is completely free and comes with zero commitment. This allows the student to experience the classroom technology and evaluate the teacher's style.

Do you provide preparation for Board exams?

Absolutely. We provide intensive preparation for IGCSE, GCSE, Pearson Edexcel, O Levels, and A Levels. This includes drilling past papers, clarifying marking schemes, and conducting timed mock exam drills.

How are lesson reports shared?

We send parent updates after sessions and compile detailed monthly progress reports detailing performance, homework tracking, and mock exam grades to keep families fully informed.
