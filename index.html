Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your online tutoring journey with Leanovia Virtual Institute.

How do I find the right online tutor?

Visit our Search Tutors page, filter by subject, grade, and availability. Browse verified profiles, then request your free demo class. We'll confirm within 24 hours.

How are online sessions conducted?

All sessions are conducted live and 1-on-1 via Zoom or Google Meet. You can join from any device — laptop, tablet, or smartphone — from anywhere in the world.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes, absolutely. Every student gets one free demo class with their chosen tutor. This lets you evaluate the teaching style and fit before any commitment. No hidden charges.

What qualifications do your tutors have?

Our tutors are highly qualified — many hold MPhil or Masters qualifications. All profiles are verified and display qualifications, experience, and teaching approach.
